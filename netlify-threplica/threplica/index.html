You add a product image. The model, the viewer, and phone AR are the rest. This page walks through that with the Timberlake chair.

Your photo

Example photo loaded. Create the 3D model to view the chair.

What you do

  1. Upload one clear product photo, or keep the Timberlake example.
  2. Press Create 3D model.
  3. Drag to rotate. On a phone, tap View in your space.

The chair on the right is the finished model from this photo. A brand-new photo needs the GPU generator, which is not connected on this public page, so only this example can be turned into a viewable model here. In-room AR scale can be adjusted, so size may not be exact.
